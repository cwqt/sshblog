Welcome to **sshblog** — a tiny blog you read over SSH.

Drop markdown files with YAML front-matter into your posts directory and they
show up here, newest first. The filename's `YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md` prefix is used
for the slug; the date and title come from the front-matter above.

Customize the title, description, key hints, and posts directory in `sshblog.yaml`.

## Navigation

- `↑`/`k` and `↓`/`j` move the cursor
- `enter` opens a post
- `/` filters the list, `r` opens the RSS feed
- `esc` goes back, `q` quits
